# SpaceShooter
A space shooter game where your ship got lost in unknown space and is under attack, how long can you survive in that chaos!?

<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d829ad11-5047-427a-b213-4e71c24be62d" />

## Controls
WASD - for movement
Esc - for Pause Menu
Space - For shooting

## What to do?
Try surviving as long as you can and gain a huge highscore by beating all the enemies.

## Death
You have five stock of health, you lose one each time you get hit and you die once you hit 0.
Also, make sure not to hit your ship into a enemy ship, suicide bombing is not good :/

### Art-Work
All the assets are designed by me, except the backgrounds, I used a free asset-pack from itch.io for those.

### Ai-Usage
I used minimal Ai, mainly for debugging in scripts, and for the variation in the shooting.
Earlier the shooting was same for every ship, I took a little help from AI for that logic and made some changes in it for my preferable results.
